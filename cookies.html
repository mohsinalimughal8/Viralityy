Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2025

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how you use the service. We also use similar technologies such as local storage and session storage.

Third-party cookies

We use Stripe for payment processing. Stripe may set cookies to prevent fraud and remember payment preferences. See Stripe's Cookie Policy.

We use Google OAuth for account sign-in. Google may set cookies as part of the authentication flow. See Google's Cookie Policy.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies from third parties.

Anonymous product usage data that helps us improve Viralityy. No personal data collected.

How to manage cookies in your browser

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling required cookies will prevent you from signing in to Viralityy. Most browsers allow you to: view stored cookies, delete specific cookies, and block cookies from specific sites.
