Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2025 · Effective: 1 May 2025

1. Overview

Viralityy ("we", "us", "our") operates an automated YouTube content creation and publishing platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share your personal information when you use our website at viralityy.com and our dashboard application.

By creating an account you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use Viralityy.

2. Data we collect

Account information

  • Name, email address, and password (hashed)
  • Payment information (processed by Stripe — we never store full card numbers)
  • Subscription plan and billing history

YouTube account data (via Google OAuth)

  • YouTube channel name, ID, and description
  • Video performance metrics (views, likes, comments, watch time)
  • OAuth access tokens (encrypted at rest)
  • We request only the minimum scopes needed: channel read access and video upload permission

Usage data

  • Dashboard interactions (pages visited, buttons clicked)
  • Video generation logs (niche, type, quality pass outcomes)
  • IP addresses (hashed before storage)
  • Browser type and device information

Content data

  • AI-generated scripts, titles and thumbnails we create on your behalf
  • Your edit requests and preview queue decisions
  • Niche selections and channel configuration

3. How we use your data

  • Delivering the service — creating, quality-checking and posting videos to your channels
  • AI optimisation — analysing your video performance to improve future content
  • Billing — processing payments and sending invoices via Stripe
  • Communications — sending transactional emails (receipts, preview notifications, password resets)
  • Fraud prevention — detecting and preventing abuse of our platform
  • Product improvement — aggregated, anonymised analytics to improve Viralityy

We do not use your data for advertising purposes and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data sharing

We share data only where necessary to operate the service:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy
  • Google / YouTube — posting videos to your channel on your behalf
  • MongoDB Atlas — database hosting (encrypted at rest)
  • Railway — server infrastructure
  • ElevenLabs — AI voiceover generation (scripts are sent; no personal data)
  • OpenAI / Anthropic — AI script generation (niche and topic data; no personal data)

All sub-processors are contractually bound to appropriate data protection standards.

5. YouTube and Google API services

Viralityy uses the YouTube Data API v3 to post videos, read channel statistics, and collect performance analytics on your behalf. Our use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically: we only use YouTube API data to provide and improve the Viralityy service. We do not use YouTube data for advertising, we do not transfer YouTube data to third parties except as required to provide the service, and we do not allow humans to read YouTube data unless you have explicitly authorised us to do so or it is required for security purposes.

You can revoke Viralityy's access to your YouTube account at any time through your Google Account permissions page.

6. Security

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS
  • Passwords hashed using bcrypt with a work factor of 12
  • OAuth tokens encrypted at rest using AES-256
  • IP addresses stored as one-way hashes (SHA-256 with a rotating salt)
  • Database access restricted to application servers only (no public access)
  • Security incidents disclosed to affected users within 72 hours where required by law

7. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Video analytics snapshots are automatically deleted after 365 days (TTL index on the database).

When you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days. Aggregated, anonymised analytics may be retained indefinitely. Billing records are retained for 7 years as required by law.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — update inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to certain types of processing
  • Restriction — request that we limit processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@viralityy.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

We use cookies for authentication (session tokens), preferences, and basic analytics. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests: privacy@viralityy.com
For general inquiries: hello@viralityy.com

We are not yet required to appoint a Data Protection Officer but take our obligations under GDPR and applicable data protection law seriously.
