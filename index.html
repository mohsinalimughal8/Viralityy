Questions

Frequently asked

Everything you need to know before starting

Do I need to film or edit anything?

No. Viralityy generates everything from scratch using AI — scripts, voiceovers, visuals, subtitles and music. You don't need a camera, microphone or any editing software.

Will YouTube flag my channel as AI-generated?

We run every video through a 6-pass quality process and automatically add YouTube's AI disclosure tag. Videos are compliant with YouTube's current policies on AI-assisted content.

How long until I see results?

Most channels start seeing consistent view growth within 3–4 weeks. The AI optimisation loop kicks in after 10–15 videos and measurably improves performance month over month.

Can I review videos before they post?

Yes. Every video goes into a 24-hour preview queue where you can approve, skip, or request edits. If you don't act, videos auto-post — or you can turn that off.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel any time from your dashboard — no questions, no penalties. Your channel content stays live on YouTube even after cancellation.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2025 · Effective: 1 May 2025

1. Overview

Viralityy operates an automated YouTube content creation and publishing platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share your personal information when you use our website and dashboard application.

2. Data we collect

We collect account information (name, email, hashed password), YouTube channel data via Google OAuth (channel name, ID, video performance metrics, OAuth tokens encrypted at rest), usage data (dashboard interactions, video generation logs, hashed IP addresses), and content data (AI-generated scripts, your edit requests and preview queue decisions).

3. How we use your data

We use your data to deliver the service, optimise AI content generation, process payments via Stripe, send transactional emails, and prevent fraud. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell your personal data.

4. YouTube API compliance

Viralityy uses YouTube Data API v3. Our use complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements. We only use YouTube data to provide and improve the Viralityy service. You can revoke access at any time via your Google Account permissions page.

5. Security

All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Passwords hashed with bcrypt. OAuth tokens encrypted at rest with AES-256. IP addresses stored as one-way SHA-256 hashes with a rotating salt.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of your personal data at any time. Email privacy@viralityy.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@viralityy.com

Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2025

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how you use the service.

Cookies we use

  • session_token (Required) — Keeps you signed in. Duration: 7 days
  • csrf_token (Required) — Protects against CSRF attacks. Duration: Session
  • ref (Required) — Tracks affiliate referral codes. Duration: 30 days
  • pref_theme (Preference) — Stores display preferences. Duration: 1 year
  • _vly_analytics (Analytics) — Anonymous product usage data. Duration: 90 days

Third-party cookies

Stripe (payment processing) and Google (OAuth sign-in) may set cookies. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies from third parties.
