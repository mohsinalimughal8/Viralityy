Questions

Why 4 plans?

4 is the proven sweet spot. We have a dedicated Shorts Pro plan because a large segment of creators only want Shorts — they don't need long-form and shouldn't pay for it. Starter is the low-barrier entry. Growth adds long-form and multi-channel. Agency scales it to 5 channels. Every plan is additive, nothing overlaps.

Does the video quota apply per channel or in total?

Per channel. If you're on Growth (3 channels, 10 Shorts/day), each of your 3 channels gets 10 Shorts — that's 30 Shorts daily in total. Agency with 5 channels gets 50 Shorts/day and 20 long-form videos/week in total. Every channel runs independently with its own niche, schedule, and posting settings.

What does the 7-day trial include?

Starter plan features: 3 Shorts per day on 1 YouTube channel, AI scripts, content quality passes, and analytics. Includes full access to the Niche Finder during the 7-day trial so you can pick your channel topic. After setup your niche is locked to the channel — changing it later depends on your plan. No credit card needed to begin. One trial per YouTube channel.

Do long-form videos post to TikTok and Instagram?

Never. Long-form videos (25–30 min) only post to YouTube — platform rules don't support them on TikTok or Instagram anyway. Auto-extracted short clips from those videos can be toggled on for TikTok and Instagram Reels. You control that per channel.

How does annual billing work?

Annual plans are billed as one upfront payment for 12 months and save you 15% compared to monthly billing. For example, Starter at $49/mo becomes $41.65/mo on annual ($499/yr). Subscriptions can be cancelled before renewal — access continues until the end of your paid period.

Can I change my channel's niche later?

You pick your niche once during setup — this is intentional. Switching niches mid-channel confuses the algorithm, resets your audience signals, and almost always reduces views. We designed the system this way to protect your channel's performance. If you genuinely need to pivot: Shorts Pro and Agency plans allow 2–3 niche changes per year. Starter plans cannot change niche after setup.

Can I upgrade or downgrade?

Yes, any time from your dashboard. Upgrades are immediate and prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. Most users start on Starter and upgrade to Growth after their first month when they see results.
